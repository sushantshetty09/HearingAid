1. System Architecture & How It Works

Hearing Aid Pro is built on a high-performance real-time digital signal processing (DSP) engine designed to amplify speech while suppressing background noise and protecting ears from dangerous acoustic peaks.

1.1 Real-Time Audio Pipeline

  1. Microphone Capture: Audio is sampled from the device microphone at 44.1 kHz, 16-bit PCM stereo via low-latency audio input streams.
  2. STFT Noise Cancellation (Ephraim-Malah Decision-Directed SNR):
    • The incoming time-domain signal is windowed using a Hann window and transformed into frequency spectrum magnitude and phase via a zero-allocation in-place Cooley-Tukey FFT (512-point STFT, 50% overlap-add).
    • Continuous Noise Estimation: Background noise profile ($\sigma_{D,k}^2$) is continuously updated during low-energy speech pauses.
    • Decision-Directed A Priori SNR ($\xi_k$): Uses previous frame clean speech power to temporally smooth signal-to-noise ratio estimates across frames ($\xi_k = \alpha \cdot \frac{|\hat{S}_{k, t-1}|^2}{\sigma_{D, k}^2} + (1 - \alpha) \cdot \max(\gamma_k - 1, 0)$ with $\alpha = 0.92$). This completely eliminates metallic "musical noise" warbling artifacts.
    • Wiener Spectral Filtering: Applies optimal spectral gain ($G_k = \frac{\xi_k}{\xi_k + 1.0}$) to attenuate noise frequencies without muffling speech formants.
  3. Multi-Band Equalizer & Dynamic Range Compression:
    • Passes audio through a 5-band biquad parametric EQ filter bank (250 Hz, 500 Hz, 1 kHz, 2 kHz, 4 kHz, 8 kHz).
    • Automatically calculates Headroom Compensation Scale to prevent digital clipping when frequency bands are boosted.
  4. Acoustic Feedback (Howling) Detector & Auto-Ducking:
    • Continuously monitors spectral energy distribution. If a single frequency bin holds >35% of total frame energy persistently (>40 ms), feedback howling is detected.
    • Automatically ducks master gain by -12 dB ($0.25\times$) instantly and displays a user notification toast, preventing ear pain from acoustic feedback loops.
  5. Soft Saturation Limiter & Peak Protection:
    • Applies a smooth $\tanh$ saturation curve above -3 dBFS with a strict safety ceiling at 0.92f (-0.7 dBFS). Unlike linear hard clipping, this guarantees safe, pleasant audio without harsh distortion.

2. Operating Procedures (How to Use)

2.1 Initial Setup

  1. 1Connect Earphones: Plug in wired headphones/earbuds or pair Bluetooth earbuds. (Do not use built-in phone speakers to avoid feedback).
  2. 2Grant Permission: On first launch, grant Microphone permission when prompted.
  3. 3Set Device Volume: Set your Android device media volume to approximately 50%.

2.2 Baseline Hearing Check (Calibration Test)

Perform the built-in 12-step hearing check in the Profile tab to calibrate the app to your hearing baseline:

  1. Navigate to the Profile tab and tap RUN BASELINE HEARING CHECK.
  2. Read the pre-test instruction disclaimer and tap Start Check.
  3. The test presents 12 steps (6 frequencies: 250 Hz, 500 Hz, 1 kHz, 2 kHz, 4 kHz, 8 kHz per ear):
    • Test tones start quiet (0.02f) and step up gradually every 750 ms.
    • Each tone includes a 10 ms linear fade-in ramp to eliminate clicks.
    • Tone volume is hard-capped at 0.35f max volume for safety.
  4. As soon as you hear the tone in the designated ear (Left or Right), tap I CAN HEAR IT. Tap Skip if inaudible, or Stop / Cancel at any time.
  5. Review your 12-threshold summary table upon test completion and tap Confirm & Save. The results automatically update your audiogram chart and sync all 12 Left/Right ear sliders.

2.3 Live Amplification & Home Tab Controls

  1. Tap the prominent Power Toggle Button on the Home tab to start or stop live audio processing.
  2. Preset Selection: Choose quick presets tailored for environments:
    • Quiet Home: Balanced mild amplification for indoor conversations.
    • Speech Clarity: Targeted high-frequency boost (2 kHz–4 kHz) for understanding voices.
    • Restaurant: Aggressive noise cancellation and mid-frequency focus for crowded venues.
    • Outdoor: Low-frequency wind noise suppression and wide dynamic range.
  3. Noise Cancellation & Equalizer: Toggle STFT noise cancellation on/off, adjust the 5-band graphic EQ sliders, or tap Suggest EQ to calculate optimal filter settings from your audiogram baseline.

2.4 Activity & Environment Tracking

Switch to the Activity tab to monitor usage and environment statistics:

  • Weekly Usage Chart: Displays active hours per day over the last 7 days, including live partial session tracking for currently running sessions.
  • Ambient Noise Level (dB) Graph: Samples ambient background noise level every 60 seconds while noise cancellation is active and renders a rolling 500-point time-series graph.
  • Environment History & Summary: Logs preset load events with timestamps and highlights your "Most Frequently Used" environment mode.

2.5 Settings & Appearance

Switch to the Settings tab for system preferences:

  • Appearance Mode: Switch between Light, Dark, or System Default themes. Theme preference is persisted and applied instantly on launch without visual flickering.
  • Ultra-Low Latency Mode: Toggle low-latency audio buffer processing (128 samples vs 512 samples) for minimal delay.
  • Data Backup & Reset: Export custom preset JSON backups or perform a full factory reset.

3. Quick Troubleshooting & Best Practices

Symptom Probable Cause Recommended Solution
High-pitched squealing sound Acoustic feedback loop (mic picking up earbud sound) Ensure earbuds are snugly fitted. Reposition phone mic away from earbuds. The auto-ducking system will lower gain by -12 dB automatically.
Harsh or distorted sound High master gain or extreme EQ boosts The built-in soft limiter protects your ears. Lower Master Gain or tap "Suggest EQ" to apply headroom-compensated curve.
Audio has slight echo/delay Standard latency audio buffer selected Open Settings tab and enable Ultra-Low Latency Mode.
No sound coming through Microphone permission denied or session off Check phone volume, ensure microphone permission is granted in Settings, and verify green active state on Home tab.
